# Bloomnote UI

Bloomnote is botanical stationery: ivory paper, deep green ink and restrained blush accents.
The garden is the visual centerpiece, with the recipient's words kept distinct from generated
flower meanings. Fraunces carries the expressive headlines and notes; DM Sans carries controls.

Open folio uses a two-column studio at 900px and above, capped at 1200px. One sheet of paper
holds the note and required names, with the garden beside it. Below that the composer puts
writing before the garden. The occasion and mood choices sit in a disclosure. Secret and
schedule controls remain available beneath the names. On phones, compact spacing and fields
of at least 16px avoid focus zoom. The fresh garden preview is labeled as an example.

Controls have quiet borders and consistent corners. Selected choices keep their labels and clear
contrast. The library uses two columns on wide screens and one on phones. Backup and restore is
reachable before the garden list. On desktop, opened gifts place the garden and the sender’s
letter side by side. On phones, the letter follows the garden and precedes the flower meanings.
The save confirmation sits in the header so it cannot cover the letter or its controls.

Dry humor belongs in supporting copy, never user-written notes, errors, dates, privacy or account
controls. Garden growth remains the main motion; reduced-motion behavior and keyboard focus remain
available. UI captures use isolated fixtures, never production account or garden writes.

The phone composer keeps the first note field inside the opening viewport. Its labeled example
preview uses the existing scene geometry at a smaller scale; generated and recipient gardens
keep their full presentation. The library gives color-based bouquet previews a separate band,
with recipient names above a quieter action row. Shared wordmarks link back to the composer.

Growing feedback retains the real status and Cancel action with a short supporting caption.
Sharing is visually primary; regrowing and starting over sit together below a divider. Empty
libraries keep one clear creation action and a small sprout.

The finished composer leads with sending and copying. A read-only link can be selected with
the keyboard if copying fails. On phones the garden comes next, followed by the recipient
preview. Preview notes and flower meanings use quieter, flatter surfaces.

Scheduled gifts and retry screens use the same stationery palette. Bloomnote’s footer leads
to its own Privacy and Terms pages, which have links to each section and to the corresponding
Accounts policy. The update prompt uses the same paper and button styles as the rest of the app.

The garden library header carries a small botanical envelope. A pressed-flower bookmark accompanies the recipient’s keepsake disclosure. Empty sent, received and device-only libraries share a compact potted-seedling vector; their copy and available actions remain distinct. These static illustrations share the stationery palette and stay separate from the person’s words and garden state. An empty library does not mount a garden scene.

Reply links retain loading failures as an explicit retry screen, rather than silently dropping the
original garden. Clear draft offers keyboard-reachable Undo, then returns focus to the note on restore.
Image export feedback belongs to the garden that started it; making another garden resets that status.
